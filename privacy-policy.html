Privacy Policy

Last updated September 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WAT Media LLC ("WAT Media," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit watmediallc.com, submit a form, work with us as a client, or communicate with us by email or text message (SMS).

Information we collect

When you fill out a form on our website or ads, request a free promo video, book a call, or become a client, we may collect:

  • Your name and business name
  • Email address and phone number
  • Business address or service area
  • Details about your business, marketing goals, and the services you're interested in
  • Account access or login invitations you choose to grant us for client work (such as ad accounts or website hosting)

We also collect standard website usage data, like pages visited, device and browser type, and how you found our site, through analytics and advertising tools such as Google Analytics and Meta (Facebook/Instagram).

How we use your information

  • Respond to inquiries and free promo video requests
  • Schedule shoots, calls, and appointments
  • Send proposals, contracts, onboarding materials, and invoices
  • Deliver and report on the services you've hired us for
  • Improve our website and measure how our own advertising performs

SMS / text messaging

If you provide your mobile number and opt in, you may receive text messages from WAT Media about your inquiry, shoot and appointment scheduling, reminders, proposals, onboarding, and client service updates.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Opting out

You can cancel SMS messages at any time. Reply STOP to the number that sent you the message. After you send "STOP," we will confirm your unsubscribe status by SMS, and you will no longer receive text messages from us. To rejoin, sign up as you did initially and we will resume sending messages.

Help

If you have trouble with our messages, reply HELP, or email us at freddie@watmediallc.com.

Message frequency and rates

Message frequency varies based on your inquiry and project schedule. Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. For questions about your text or data plan, contact your wireless provider.

How we share information

We do not sell or rent your personal information, including your mobile number. We share information only with service providers that help us run the business, such as our CRM and messaging platform, form and email providers, website hosting, and analytics tools, and only to deliver the services described here. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Cookies and tracking

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies for analytics and to measure and improve our advertising. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can adjust ad preferences in your Google and Meta account settings.

Data retention

We keep your information as long as needed to respond to you, provide our services, and meet legal and recordkeeping obligations. You can ask us to delete your information at any time by emailing us.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

Children's privacy

Our services are for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
