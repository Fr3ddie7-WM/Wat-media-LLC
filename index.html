Why would we shoot a $1,500 video for free?

Because I'm tired of watching great local businesses lose to competitors with bigger marketing budgets. I grew up in the Shenandoah Valley. I played college football and learned that you earn trust through performance — not promises. If you just want the video and nothing else, it's yours. No pressure. No invoice. That's how I want to do business.

The Wat Promise

If you're not thrilled with your free promo video, we'll reshoot it — at no cost, no questions asked. We don't invoice for work that doesn't speak for itself.

Seriously — it's actually free? What's the catch?
Yes, 100% free. We shoot it, edit it, deliver it, and you keep it forever — whether you ever work with us again or not. The "catch" is that we're betting you'll like what we do and want to keep going. But we never pressure anyone. The video is yours unconditionally.
What if I just want the free video and nothing else?
Then you get a free professional video and we part as friends. Seriously — this happens, and we're completely fine with it. We'd rather have a great video floating around the Valley with our work on it than force a relationship that isn't right. The only thing we ask: if you love it, tell someone.
How do you make money if the video is free?
Most clients see the quality of their video and want to keep working together — running Meta ads, managing their social media, or shooting more content. That's where we build a real business relationship. But we never pressure you. Ever. The free video is how we earn the right to have that conversation.
What types of businesses do you work with?
Local businesses in the Shenandoah Valley — restaurants, contractors, gyms, farms, shops, service providers, venues, medical practices, and more. If you have a business that local people should know about, we want to help you get found.
How long does the whole process take?
From form submission to finished video is typically 7–14 days. We'll schedule the shoot at your convenience, film in a few hours, and deliver the edited video within a week of the shoot day.
